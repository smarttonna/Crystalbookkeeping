Hi, I Am Wuraola Oluwalana

I run Crystal Bookkeeping and work exclusively with service-based businesses globally.

I went into bookkeeping because I realized something: I was more curious about the numbers behind a business than the numbers in experiments. Today, I hold an Intuit bookkeeping certification and work daily in tools like Zoho Books, QuickBooks Online, and Xero — building systems that give business owners real clarity: not just clean books, but books that tell them something useful.

Certification & Rigorous Standards

These are real credentials earned through structured training programs with formal assessments — not participation badges or honorary titles. The most prominently featured is the Intuit Bookkeeping Certification, which covers foundational accounting principles and real business applications. It is the credential I point to first because it reflects the quality standard behind everything I deliver.

Core Bookkeeping Responsibilities Managed:

  • Manage complete bookkeeping cycles using QuickBooks Online, Xero, Zoho Books.
  • Perform bank and credit card reconciliations across varying transaction volumes.
  • Record adjusting journal entries, accruals, deferrals, and revenue recognition.
  • Prepare monthly financial statements (Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow).
  • Conduct ledger reviews, historical clean-up, and discrepancy correction.
  • Ensure financial records remain organized, compliant, and audit-ready.
