The Cleanup Process

What happens when you work with us

Once you approve the quote and pay deposit, work begins promptly. Cleanup engagements typically run 3 to 15 business days depending on scope.

1

Assessment & Proposal

Full QBO review. Scenario identified. Written cleanup plan and fixed price sent for your approval before work begins.

2

Document Collection

Bank statements, credit card statements, Amazon settlement CSVs, Shopify exports, and supplier invoices collected upfront.

3

Foundation & Data Import

Chart of Accounts rebuilt for your business. Erroneous entries purged first. Historical data pushed oldest first and verified in batches.

4

Error Correction

Duplicates, uncategorized transactions, sales tax reclassification, personal expenses, and product purchases systematically corrected.

5

Full Reconciliation

Every bank account, credit card, and clearing account for every month in the cleanup period reconciled to zero discrepancy.

6

Review & Sanity Check

P&L, Balance Sheet, and Transaction Detail reviewed. Gross margin sanity check against known product costs prior to final delivery.

7

Delivery & Virtual Walkthrough

Full reports package, KPI dashboard, virtual walkthrough session, and complete handover report delivered to you with complete peace of mind.
